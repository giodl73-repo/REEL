## Retained tension

Expressive travel remains optional; restrained holds and reduced motion preserve information. Sound can motivate a cut, while narration or a reading pause can justify holding it. Technical geometry and cadence checks support visual judgment without determining creative quality.

## Disposition

All major planning findings have an owner and an explicit acceptance gate. The plan is ready for execution. Completion requires implementation and qualification evidence; this review grants no creative selection or release approval.
