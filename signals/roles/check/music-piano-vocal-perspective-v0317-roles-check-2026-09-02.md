# REEL v0.3.17 piano/vocal and dual-perspective roles check

## Artifact identification

- Type: additive schema, Rust implementation, CLI, deterministic export/report,
  synthetic tests, and documentation.
- Domain: music reconstruction, piano/vocal notation, model comparison,
  provenance, privacy, external-adapter boundaries, and musician review.
- Reviewed paths: `crates/reel-music/src/model.rs`,
  `crates/reel-music/src/perspective.rs`, `src/music_score.rs`, CLI wiring,
  tests, and `docs/music-piano-vocal-perspective-v0.3.17.md`.

## Role selection

- Music Reconstruction Engineer: verifies immutable model identity, musical
  timebase alignment, deterministic comparison, and score evidence.
- Score and Arrangement Director: verifies piano reduction semantics, notation
  utility, parts, measures, and human arrangement authority.
- Sound Designer: verifies the relationship between audible source evidence,
  piano interpretation, rehearsal output, and listening limits.
- Rights and Provenance Steward: verifies model/license/egress boundaries,
  privacy, authority, and separation of technical and human gates.
- Platform and Audience: verifies that the delivered notation format is useful
  to its actual singer/pianist audience and that missing delivery formats remain
  explicit.

## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 5
P1 blockers: 0  |  P2 issues: 0  |  P3 notes: 20

Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS**

Top finding: the feature safely creates and verifies an editable measured
piano/vocal score, but it must not be described as engraved or musician-ready
until real notation applications and a human singer/pianist review it.

Cross-role consensus: the piano reduction is valuable as a second governed
perspective, but remains a dependent arrangement candidate whose agreement with
the source cannot create creative authority or independent proof.

## Amendments

1. Add an independent semantic re-import check for measured piano/vocal
   MusicXML before claiming lossless notation round trip.
2. Add a hash-pinned local notation adapter and two-application interoperability
   test before offering PDF/SVG or “musician-ready” delivery.
3. Benchmark owner-selected piano-generation adapters on synthetic or cleared
   audio, retaining exact model/license/settings evidence and human A/B review.
