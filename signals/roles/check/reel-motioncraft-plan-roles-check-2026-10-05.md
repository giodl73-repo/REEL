## Disagreements retained

- Animation direction benefits from expressive entrances; platform review
  requires a reduced-motion option. Resolution: expressive motion remains
  available in the main treatment; the alternate preserves claims and holds
  with restrained motion. Neither is a universal default.
- The editor favors beat-motivated cuts; story and sound review allow an
  explanatory pause to override musical regularity. Resolution: every cut
  names its motivation; beat alignment is optional and silence is authored.
- Automated geometry can reject clipping, but typography hierarchy and
  comprehension require visual judgment. Resolution: structural diagnostics
  and creative findings are separate; no automatic artistic pass.
