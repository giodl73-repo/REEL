# Native execution of a rooted comic part repair

The [rooted repair fixture](2026-11-19-rooted-part-repair.md) qualifies local
deterministic issue replay and private plan/draft export. Its pending declarations
are not native input admission. The next opt-in fixture, `repair_part_native`,
replaces the opening part's declaration with a real exact Git/Tig snapshot and
executes only that root under the complete scoped repair plan.

It reads the original part through its exact native snapshot and retained proof,
checks every proposed replacement against the qualified candidate's pins and
authors seven explicitly scoped `.tig` records with exact annex-leaf bindings.
The new source commit, metadata-only snapshot correspondence and authored comic
binding are checked before closing any job. The new part explicitly chooses its
own pinned runtime; the previous operation's runtime is never adopted or changed.

The completed whole-issue report releases its slot only through the original
runtime's `prepare-close` and `close-job` transaction into retained coverage.
The new part has a distinct operation, attempt, activation and WorkRef in that
same enrolled slot. No actor is enrolled and the other protected Waiting job
must reopen. Changed input bytes receive durable declared protection before
native publication/reopen. Native dispatch then stages the admitted source,
renders, independently checks outputs, retains them and completes the journal.

Qualification must check actual native result/owner proof, changed reading
pixels, unchanged clean art, exact editable output correspondence with the local
candidate, stable page/panel/balloon IDs, the untouched closing task/packet/history,
historical report access after slot reuse and replay with an unchanged actual
Index checkpoint. Static `claims.json` equality alone is not that evidence.

The fixture writes exact source/admission/packet/operation handles before effects,
plus phase, close ticket, receipt, per-input publication reports, handoff, worker
and retained result controls. An interrupted run must reconcile those same
handles. Do not rerun the initial fixture into a fresh root or substitute runtime
or operation identity after any uncertain close, admission or publication.
Owned source/work/ingress directories are preserved on failure and success.

Full corrected-issue assembly is still separate work: derive fresh control inputs
from the new retained opening and old exact closing, admit/retain the new assembly
and generate a complete contextual review. Other descendant declarations remain
explicitly pending and cannot authorize production. Part review controls do not
stand in for the full issue. No model call, tracker effect, finding closure,
selection, scratch retirement, real BBJ/Melena adoption or S5 scale is implied.

Producer-applied comics-editor and rights/provenance checklists preserve Spanish
source text and original IDs, exact history, installed-font restrictions, private
technical derivatives and separate creative/contextual approval. They are not an
independent review.
