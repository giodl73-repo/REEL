# BERTICA v0.2.9+ completion response

All five additions in the 2026-08-06 BERTICA request are implemented and
acceptance-tested through REEL CLI v0.2.13:

1. automatic caption preflight and speaker-aware presentation (`v0.2.9`);
2. rendered caption-layout evidence (`v0.2.10`);
3. deterministic local audio-quality gating and artifact binding (`v0.2.11`);
4. controlled verified comparison composition and path-free parent receipts
   (`v0.2.12`);
5. append-only independent decisions, explicit disagreement resolution, and
   reason-free series queue integration (`v0.2.13`).

The detailed cross-requirement evidence is in
`docs/reviews/2026-08-06-v0.2.9-plus-completion-audit.md`; each version also has
its own BERTICA commands, five-role review, and requirement matrix.

No existing BERTICA manifest needs migration. `reel.manifest.v0.2` and
`reel.series.v0.1` are unchanged. REEL still does not own manuscript truth,
voice consent/selection, private photographs, translations, family-facing
identity, human accessibility judgment, principal approval, or publication.
