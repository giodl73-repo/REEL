Author-applied inspection using [REEL's role rules](../../.roles/ROLE.md),
not independent review or human production approval. Scope: shared contracts,
manual prompt export and byte-checked candidate return, plus abstract lifecycle
journal contracts. All fixtures are synthetic.

| Lens | Finding and disposition |

| [Platform and Audience](../../.roles/platform-audience.md) | A CLI success could imply a usable publication. Capabilities and documentation limit this slice to manual exchange. Media format, audience delivery, captions and actual-use credits are not validated by hashes. |

The main retained tension is early usability versus production custody. W1 is
usable for preparing and returning manual work, while candidates remain at
caller-owned paths and require later verification/admission. The journal tests
exercise fake adapters; no native qualification follows from them. Production
film acceptance remains gated by the later film-first plan requirements.

Changed/partial candidate controls stop without repair. Capture streams actual
bytes within authored output bounds and exact replay preserves control files.
Windows held handles deny sharing writes during capture; portable checks do not
establish immutable external custody. No existing project profiles, approvals,
media, selected recordings or active jobs are modified by this slice.
