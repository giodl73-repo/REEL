# v0.2.11 audio-quality role review

| Lens | Decision | Evidence | Residual boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animation Director | Pass | Audio gating is additive lineage and does not change frames, motion, captions, or timing. | Visual approval is unaffected. |
| Editor | Pass | Optional manifest comparison blocks audio/timeline mismatch at 50 ms before rendering. | REEL does not choose a take or edit. |
| Platform and Audience | Pass | Four explicit profiles record every loudness, peak, channel, rate, silence, and margin threshold. | Profiles are technical gates, not listener or accessibility approval. |
| Sound Designer | Pass | EBU R128, true/sample peak, peak counts, silence ranges, stream facts, and stem margin are measured and hash-bound. | REEL does not normalize, repair, mix, or master audio. |
| Story Director | Pass | Strict JSON omits paths, transcripts, names, and manuscript references. | Voice consent, identity, performance, and approval remain with BERTICA. |

Panel decision: pass. The gate is deterministic, local, read-only, path-free,
tamper-evident, optional at render time, and leaves `reel.manifest.v0.2`
unchanged.
