# v0.2.8 caption accessibility role review

Review date: 2026-08-05

| Lens | Decision | Evidence | Residual boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animation Director | Pass | The audit runs before rendering and does not alter composition, motion, or protected regions. | Caption placement against faces and visual focus still needs frame review. |
| Editor | Pass | Cue duration, overlap, line density, and reading speed make pacing failures actionable by cue index. | The tool does not decide whether wording or cuts serve the scene. |
| Platform and Audience | Pass | Configurable line, duration, and reading-speed limits support phone and sound-off review without exposing text. | Font size, contrast, safe area, device legibility, and platform-specific policy remain separate checks. |
| Sound Designer | Pass | Timing metrics expose captions that cannot comfortably track narration or silence. | The audit does not evaluate mix, performance, translation, or caption/audio semantic agreement. |
| Story Director | Pass | Reports omit dialogue and source identity, so the audit cannot silently publish story content. | Canon, adaptation fidelity, and emotional phrasing remain human decisions. |

Panel decision: pass. The five repository roles support an opt-in v0.2.8 gate
with explicit policy and a clear rendered-pixel boundary. The manifest contract
does not change.
