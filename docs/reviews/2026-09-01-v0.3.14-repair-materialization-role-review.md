# v0.3.14 deterministic repair-materialization role review

Artifact type: repair schema extension, raw-PCM renderer, CLI, receipts,
continuity evidence, documentation, and synthetic tests.

Selected roles: Music Reconstruction Engineer, Sound Designer, Editor, Score
and Arrangement Director, and Rights and Provenance Steward. Together they
cover sample identity, audible transitions, edit semantics, score-grid
inheritance, private inputs, and approval boundaries.

## Music Reconstruction Engineer

| # | Finding | Severity | Section | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every operation resolves against immutable source coordinates, avoiding order-dependent drift. | P3 | materializer | Preserve source-coordinate semantics. |
| 2 | Unchanged spans are copied as original bytes and carry source/output range hashes. | P3 | lock evidence | Keep exact identity mandatory. |
| 3 | Legacy cut-only EDL output is untouched while the additive materializer covers the full vocabulary. | P3 | compatibility | Retain the existing legacy hash tests. |

## Sound Designer

## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 5

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 0 | P3 notes: 15

Verdict: APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS

Top finding: full operation support is credible only when unchanged-region
identity and seam evidence are verified from the rendered bytes.

Cross-role consensus: deterministic edit semantics, explicit musical grids,
path-free lineage, and separate human listening/selection gates are mandatory.

   rather than implying an automatic creative repair. Incorporated in the
