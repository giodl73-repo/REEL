The real proof exposed three useful pitfalls. Pitch ledger lines share the main
staff CSS class, so pagination identifies the full-width five-line grid while
retaining every ledger line. Two-font PDF conversion allocates auxiliary font
objects in randomized order; canonical traversal of the reachable PDF graph
removes incidental byte revisions without rewriting drawing/font streams.
Source text bodies need not include their title, so explicit retained metadata
is the label authority rather than a first-line heuristic.

Producer-applied REEL score/arrangement, reconstruction, sound, editing and
rights/provenance checks preserve notation and canonical wording, keep the
diagnostic study distinct from a master, enforce explicit font inputs and private
scope, and separate technical checks from release authority. Melena identity,
placement, custody, concurrency, privacy and Git/adoption checks keep stable IDs,
all retained subjects and old packages, avoid cache initialization/eviction/global
locks, and require qualified runtime adoption separately. No independent reviewer
or creative approval is claimed. Hosted qualification remains pending.
