# Native prefix composition: producer-applied review

Review applies the Editor, Sound Designer and Rights and Provenance Steward
checklists in `.roles/ROLE.md`. It is producer-applied technical review, with
separate independent code review; no human creative approval is inferred.

Editing: the complete prefix and complete original body retain immutable source
proofs and local clocks. The compiler derives the new programme placements. It
rejects subsets, caller offsets and incompatible streams, and checks complete
ordered decoded pictures and exact copied H264 packet payloads. The concat path
disables automatic Annex-B conversion when making the packet identity claim.

Sound: the browser master uses a selected original PCM window, source-clock
anchors and deterministic optional sample envelope. Full original sample count,
selected PCM and enveloped PCM identities are checked. Composition preserves
all original PCM parts and makes one continuous MP3 encode. MP3 remains lossy;
its bounded codec tail is not PCM equality. Declared padding and holds must be
zero. Boundary measurements do not confer listening approval.

Rights/provenance: the historical producer is accepted only through the
explicit bounded composition contract and its staged executable identity.
Ordinary v2 finalization retains its current executable gate. The v3 receipt
separates original proof identities/clocks, effective placements and actual
finalizer identity. Retained lossless cache references are not presented as
freshly read bytes. Original source-clock evidence, caption words and selection
records remain hash-bound; output stays `not-authorized`.

Browser frame capture retains its existing renderer and default review mode.
Master mode binds fonts and all source assets, waits for fonts, and exposes the
same nearest rational source-clock anchors to deterministic frame events.
Musical note choice, typography, cut rhythm, rights and whole programme
acceptance require their actual production gates.
