Original renderer identity and current checker identity are distinct. The strict
entry point stays unchanged. An explicit reuse entry point independently admits
the original producer for this reuse, recomputes current consumed meaning under
that producer pin, and compares the unchanged selected subject with its original
replayed claim. It neither executes an old producer by pretending to be that host
nor declares different producers equivalent. Interpreter/source/vendor hashes,
operation semantics/parameters, snapshot, binding/admission and current byte checks
stay strict. Unsupported original interpreters remain unsupported.

may differ from the retained output recipe. Both fingerprints remain separate;
source nodes, binding, admission, checkpoint and operation parameters must agree
when replanning. Grouped ownership must match the checker job, not relabel the old
output as newly produced. Caller must explicitly authorize the retained producer;
historical replay alone cannot grant current reuse or creative approval.
