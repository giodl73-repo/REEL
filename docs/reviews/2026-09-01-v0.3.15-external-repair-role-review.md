checker, CLI, documentation, and synthetic pass/fail evidence.

Selected roles: Music Reconstruction Engineer, Sound Designer, Editor, Lyrics
and Vocal Adaptation Editor, and Rights and Provenance Steward.

## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 5

P1 blockers: 0 | P2 issues: 0 | P3 notes: 15

Verdict: APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS

Top finding: generated target text cannot validate the returned performance;
candidate-bound independent lyric evidence is mandatory.

Cross-role consensus: exact edit boundaries, outside-region identity, consent,
model provenance, and separate listening/selection gates must remain coupled.

## Amendments

1. Reject self-authored lyric evidence and require exact candidate/text hashes.
   Incorporated in the candidate checker.
2. Require complete-timeline output with exact bytes outside the requested
   region. Incorporated with synthetic tampering and rejected-candidate tests.
3. Forbid execution, downloads, network access, upload, release, and inferred
   consent in the adapter contract. Incorporated in request validation.
