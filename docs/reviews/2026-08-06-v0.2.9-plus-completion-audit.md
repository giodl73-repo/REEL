## Contract and migration audit

- `reel.manifest.v0.2` is byte-for-byte unchanged across v0.2.9–v0.2.13.
- `reel.series.v0.1` is unchanged; decision integration is an optional separate
  `reel.review-index.v0.1` input.
- Existing five BERTICA manifests require no migration.
- Existing v0.2.9+ animatic artifacts remain accepted. v0.2.12 narrows only the
  verifier's numeric comparison for JSON-round-tripped caption metrics to
  `1e-9`; hashes, cue identities, policy, geometry, events, and violation
  structure remain exact.
- Privacy-safe animatic receipts remain strict v0.1 and path-free. New caption
  images, audio reports, comparison decode artifacts, and decision reasons are
  private unless deliberately shared through their separate safe derivative.
