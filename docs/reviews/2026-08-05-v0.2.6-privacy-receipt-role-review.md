| Lens | Decision | Evidence | Residual boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animation Director | Pass | The receipt carries normalized motion method, shot count, and aggregate transform-safety result without scene or shot identities. | It does not communicate artistic intent or composition quality. |
| Editor | Pass | Duration, frame rate, motion shot count, and caption count remain inspectable. | It intentionally omits edit structure and cannot substitute for editorial review. |

| Story Director | Pass | Work IDs, titles, source references, narration, people, and approval records are omitted. | The receipt cannot establish canon or adaptation quality. |

| CI Gate Auditor | Pass | Hosted CI generates the receipt after real verification, asserts binding hashes/fingerprint, and rejects path-bearing tokens. | The negative scan complements rather than replaces typed schema review. |
| Public Surface Scrubber | Pass | No work ID, filename, path, input ID/hash, executable string, shot ID, or unknown raw input kind is serialized. | Sharing remains an intentional human action and does not imply consent or release approval. |

Panel decision: pass for a v0.2.6 review candidate. The command creates a useful
operational proof while keeping the path-rich source artifact in its proper
local verification boundary.
