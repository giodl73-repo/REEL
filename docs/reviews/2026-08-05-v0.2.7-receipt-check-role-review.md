# v0.2.7 receipt-check role review

The role panel reviewed the recipient-side verification boundary. The panel
passes the change with `reel.manifest.v0.2` unchanged.

- **Animation:** normalized motion and aggregate safety are checked; artistic
  motion quality is not certified.
- **Editor:** CFR and duration are measured; rhythm, shot order, and editorial
  intent are outside the receipt.
- **Platform:** codec, pixel format, dimensions, CFR, bytes, and audio state are
  checked; burned-caption wording and device legibility are not.
- **Sound:** audio-stream presence is checked; mix and voice quality are not.
- **Story:** the path-free report exposes no work identity or source detail and
  makes no canon claim.
- **Maintainer:** strict unknown-field rejection prevents schema smuggling;
  adding receipt fields requires an explicit schema revision.
- **CI:** real generation and verification cover success, injected-field
  rejection, and wrong-video hashing. This is integrity evidence, not signing.
- **Public scrubber:** output is limited to hashes and delivery measurements;
  paths, names, and identity are absent.
