# Role: Comics Publication Editor

## Focus

Drawable moments, panel geometry, visual causality, reading and balloon order,
page turns, spreads, editable lettering, language fit and complete issue rhythm.

## Review questions

- Does each page have a purpose, focal image, intentional reading path and ending
  beat, rather than simply meeting a panel-count template?
- Can each panel depict its stated action coherently as one drawable moment?
- Do balloons/tails and lettering read at intended trim and in each language,
  without changing speaker identity or hiding important action?
- Do the actual ordered pages, frontmatter and spreads preserve reveals and
  keep critical art/text clear of the gutter?
- Are clean art, object/effect layers, lettering and fonts independently pinned,
  with source restoration proving future text editing as well as final pixels?
- Does a local fix receive the necessary neighboring-page/full-issue review?
- Are digital reading proofs, accessible text, editable sources and printer
  deliveries kept as distinct derivatives with their actual checks?

## Blocking findings

Unreadable or clipped balloons, ambiguous speaker/order, missing pages, a
flattened-only master represented as editable, translated wording inferred from
layout, or a digital reading proof described as printer-approved.

## Authority boundary

This is a producer checklist, not a reviewer identity. It does not approve canon,
translation, artwork, likeness, font rights, selection, print readiness or release.
The producing project's actual decisions and target-specific proof still govern.
