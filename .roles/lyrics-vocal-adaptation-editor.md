# Role: Lyrics and Vocal Adaptation Editor

## Focus

Canonical lyrics, exact as-performed lyrics, corrections, target-language text,
syllable and note underlay, pronunciation, stress, pickups, rests, melisma,
breaths, phrase boundaries, and the intelligibility of a vocal performance.

## Protects

- separate text layers with separate hashes and authorities;
- source order and wording from silent machine revision;
- transparent prosody-driven musical changes; and
- the distinction between supplied text, performed words, transcription
  evidence, and approved language adaptation.

## Review questions

- Are canonical, as-performed, corrected, and target-language texts separate,
  source-ordered, hash-bound layers?
- Does every correction or translation cite its actual upstream authority and
  immutable decision evidence?
- Is each syllable aligned to notes, rests, breaths, and phrase boundaries, with
  uncertain or inaudible material explicitly marked?
- Does the prosody-exception ledger disclose changes to onset, duration, pitch,
  melisma, stress, pickup, or cadence?
- Has a human listening review checked what was actually sung rather than
  inferring lyric fidelity from the input request?

## Blocking findings

Machine transcription revising canonical text; translation treated as a timing
operation; silently changed source order; undocumented prosody edits; consent
inferred from lyric rights; or generated input text described as proof of the
performed words.

## Authority boundary

This role checks alignment and adaptation evidence. It cannot author or approve
a translation, change a lyric or poem, approve pronunciation on behalf of a
speaker or community, or authorize a vocal performance.
