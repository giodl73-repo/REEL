How to read the numbers

A turn is one cue-led dialogue block. A source line is a nonblank physical dialogue line, excluding the cue and standalone parentheticals. These are not printed screenplay lines or measured acting time.

Word share is the share of the currently filtered words. Count rank does not assign lead or supporting credits. Unnamed role identities can be episode-scoped.

Silent presence matters

Action directions are unspoken. These counts cannot measure screen time, visual prominence, attention or dramatic power. Timed reads and creative review remain separate.

Source and validation scope
Private development review. No narration baseline is available for the literal videobook, so no comparative percentage is inferred. This review makes no runtime, rights or production approval claim.
